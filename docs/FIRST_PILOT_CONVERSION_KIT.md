# First Pilot Conversion Kit

## Two Gates Before a Pilot Starts

### Gate 1: Qualification Before Demo

Ask these during the first reply or at the start of the demo. Takes 90 seconds.

| Question | Good Answer | Red Flag |
|---|---|---|
| "כמה משרות מפרסמים בפייסבוק בשבוע?" | 3+ per week | "We don't really post on Facebook" |
| "כמה קבוצות?" | 5+ groups | "Just 1–2" or "I don't know" |
| "מי עושה את הפרסום?" | Named person (ideally on this call) | "We outsource it" or "nobody specifically" |

**Pass gate 1 if:** they post to FB groups regularly AND the person on the call is the one who posts (or manages the person who does).

**Fail gate 1:** politely end. "Sounds like Facebook groups aren't a big channel for you right now. If that changes, reach out — we'll be here."

### Gate 2: Qualification Before Pilot

Ask at the end of the demo, before offering the pilot.

| Question | Good Answer | Red Flag |
|---|---|---|
| "יש לכם משרה פתוחה שנוכל לפרסם ביחד היום?" | Yes, specific role | "Not right now" or "maybe next week" |
| "מוכנים להשקיע 15 דקות להתחלה?" | Yes | "I need to check with my boss" (for a free pilot) |
| "אפשר לשלוח לי רשימת קבוצות שאתם מפרסמים בהן?" | Yes, can send quickly | "I'll get to it sometime" |

**Pass gate 2 if:** they have a live vacancy AND are willing to start within 48 hours.

**Fail gate 2:** don't force it. "No rush. When you have a vacancy ready, ping me and we'll set up in 15 minutes."

## What Makes a Lead Worth a 14-Day Pilot

| Good Pilot Candidate | Bad Pilot Candidate |
|---|---|
| Posts to FB groups at least weekly | Doesn't post to FB groups at all |
| Has 3+ open vacancies right now | No current openings |
| Decision-maker is on the call | "I need to ask my manager" (for a free trial) |
| Can name specific FB groups they use | Vague about their process |
| Willing to start in 1–2 days | "Maybe in a few weeks" |
| Shows curiosity about tracking/results | Only cares about "does it auto-post?" |
| Small enough that owner decides (3–15 people) | Requires procurement or committee |

## What the Founder Should Ask For Before Starting

Send this after they agree to pilot:

```
מעולה! כדי להכין הכל, אני צריך 3 דברים:

1️⃣ רשימת קבוצות פייסבוק שאתם מפרסמים בהן
   (קישורים או שמות — מה שנוח)

2️⃣ משרה פתוחה לפרסום ראשון
   כותרת + מיקום + דרישות + שכר + דרך הגשה

3️⃣ 15 דקות לשיחת הדרכה — מתי נוח?

זה הכל. אני מכין ונפרסם ביחד.
```

**Minimum viable:** if they only send group names + a job title, that's enough. You can extract details during the onboarding call.

## How to Push for a Concrete Next Step

### After Demo — They're Interested but Not Committing

**Soft push:**
```
מה הצעד הבא מבחינתך?
```

**Medium push:**
```
אפשר לעשות את זה ככה: שלח לי רשימת קבוצות ומשרה, אני מכין הכל, ומפרסמים ביחד מחר. 15 דקות.
```

**Direct push:**
```
הפיילוט חינם ולוקח 15 דקות להתחיל. אני יכול להעלות אותך עכשיו. רוצה?
```

### After Demo — They Said "I Need to Think"

**Probe first:**
```
מה הדבר העיקרי שאתה רוצה לוודא?
```

**Then based on their answer:**

| They Say | You Say |
|---|---|
| "I need to check with my team" | "הבנתי. רוצה שאכין סיכום קצר שתוכל להעביר? או שאעשה דמו מהיר גם להם?" |
| "I need to see if it's worth the time" | "הפיילוט הוא בדיוק לזה — 14 יום לראות אם זה חוסך זמן. בלי סיכון." |
| "I'm worried about security/data" | "אנחנו לא ניגשים לפייסבוק שלך. לא סיסמאות. לא הרשאות. רק מייצרים טקסט ועוקבים אחרי תוצאות." |
| "I'm not sure Facebook groups work for us" | "בדיוק בשביל זה הפיילוט — 14 יום ותדע עם נתונים. לא עם תחושה." |

## How to Close Softly Without Sounding Needy

### Do
- Give them a clear, easy next step: "שלח לי רשימת קבוצות ואני מכין"
- Set a specific time: "מתי נוח ל-15 דקות מחר?"
- Restate the risk-free nature: "חינם, בלי התחייבות"
- Create light urgency: "אני לוקח 20 סוכנויות בשלב הראשון"

### Don't
- Don't follow up more than 3 times without a response
- Don't say "just checking in" — always add value or a specific reason
- Don't discount further than the early-adopter offer
- Don't say "I really need your feedback" — it signals desperation
- Don't apologize for reaching out — you're offering something valuable

### The "Back Door" Close
If they're clearly interested but not ready to commit:
```
אגיד ככה — אני אשמור לך מקום בפיילוט. כשתהיה מוכן, תכתוב לי ואני אעלה אותך תוך 15 דקות. בלי לחץ.
```
This works because it removes pressure while keeping them in your pipeline with agency.

## Pilot Kick-Off Micro-Checklist

When they say yes, run through this in 15 minutes:

```
□ Got their group list (even 5 groups is enough to start)
□ Got one vacancy to post
□ Created their account
□ Loaded their groups into directory
□ Generated first AI post together (they see the magic moment)
□ Posted to 2–3 groups together (they see the speed)
□ Showed them results tracking ("update this tomorrow with how many responses you got")
□ Confirmed WhatsApp support channel
□ Set Day 3 check-in reminder
□ Logged in pilot tracking sheet
```

**If they can't provide groups/vacancy right now:**
```
"No problem — send me whenever you're ready and I'll have everything prepared in 10 minutes. Just WhatsApp me."
```
Set a follow-up for tomorrow if they don't send by end of day.
