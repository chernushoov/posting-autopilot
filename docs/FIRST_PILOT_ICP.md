# First Pilot ICP — Ideal First Customer Profile

---

## The Perfect First Pilot Agency

An Israeli recruitment agency that:
- Has 3–15 recruiters
- Posts vacancies to Facebook groups as a core sourcing channel
- Does it manually (copy-paste) or with a cheap autoposter they're nervous about
- Has no real tracking on which groups produce results
- Is operationally chaotic enough to feel the pain, but organized enough to complete a 14-day pilot
- Has a decision-maker (owner or ops manager) reachable via WhatsApp

---

## ICP Dimensions

### Agency Size

| Size | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 person (freelance recruiter) | ❌ Low | Too small to pay. Useful for testimonials only. |
| 2–5 recruiters | ✅ Good | Feels the pain. Owner makes decisions fast. Low friction. |
| 5–15 recruiters | ✅✅ Best | Pain is acute. Multiple people wasting time. Budget exists. Quick decision. |
| 15–30 recruiters | ✅ Good | Bigger deal size. But slower decision, may need "committee." |
| 30+ recruiters | ⚠️ Later | Enterprise motion. Long sales cycle. Not for wave 1. |

**Sweet spot for wave 1: 3–15 recruiters.**

### Posting Behavior

| Signal | Fit Level | How to Detect |
|---|---|---|
| Posts to Facebook groups daily | ✅✅ Best | Their posts visible in groups |
| Posts to Facebook groups weekly | ✅ Good | Less frequent but still a user |
| Posts only to their own Facebook page | ⚠️ Weak | Different use case — not group posting |
| Doesn't use Facebook at all | ❌ No fit | Not our customer |
| Uses an autoposter | ✅✅ Best | High urgency — switch before ban |
| Had a Facebook ban/restriction | ✅✅✅ Perfect | Maximum pain. Maximum urgency. |

**Best signal: you can find their posts in Facebook groups right now.**

### Sectors They Recruit For

| Sector | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tech (software, IT, QA, DevOps) | ✅✅ Best | Highest volume of FB group posting in Israel. Many active groups. |
| Blue collar (construction, logistics, manufacturing) | ✅ Good | Heavy FB group usage. Less tech-savvy — need easy UX. |
| Service (restaurants, retail, hospitality) | ✅ Good | High volume, high turnover, lots of FB posting. |
| Finance / legal / senior executive | ⚠️ Weak | LinkedIn-first. Less FB group activity. |
| Healthcare / nursing | ✅ Good | Active FB groups for nursing/caretaker roles. |

**Wave 1 priority: Tech + Blue Collar + Service agencies.**

### Geography

| Location | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tel Aviv / Gush Dan | ✅✅ Best | Highest density of agencies. Most FB group activity. |
| Haifa / North | ✅ Good | Active market, less competition. |
| Jerusalem | ✅ Good | Specific groups, some unique dynamics. |
| Beer Sheva / South | ✅ Good | Smaller but underserved. |
| Fully remote agencies | ✅ Good | No geographic constraint. |

**Don't filter by geography in wave 1 — any Israeli agency that posts to FB groups is a fit.**

### Channel Usage

| Channel Behavior | What It Tells You |
|---|---|
| Active in multiple FB groups | Core user. High pain. Best prospect. |
| Posts in Hebrew with emojis and formatting | Cares about post quality. Will value AI generation. |
| Posts plain text copy-paste (identical post everywhere) | Using templates. Will value AI variety + tracking. |
| Has a Facebook business page with job posts | Invested in Facebook. Will see value in group expansion. |
| Active on LinkedIn but not Facebook | Not our wave 1 customer. |
| Active on Telegram HR channels | May be reachable via Telegram. Secondary channel. |

## Signs They'll Value This Product

### Strong Positive Signals (Prioritize These)
1. **Posting identical text to multiple groups** — they're doing manual copy-paste
2. **Posting at scale (5+ groups per vacancy)** — high time investment, high pain
3. **Posts are low quality / generic** — they'll love AI-generated content
4. **No tracking visible** — posting same vacancy repeatedly without knowing results
5. **Complaining about Facebook in forums/groups** — bans, restrictions, slow growth
6. **Using known autoposter tools** — high urgency to switch before ban
7. **Multiple recruiters posting from same agency** — multiplied pain, multiplied value

### Moderate Signals
8. **Agency website lists "Facebook" as a channel** — they're invested
9. **Active on AllJobs/Drushim AND Facebook** — multi-channel, organized enough to pilot
10. **Recently posted urgent/high-volume vacancies** — acute need for speed

## Who to Avoid in Wave 1

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| **Large agencies (30+)** | Slow decisions. Procurement process. Need enterprise sales motion. |
| **Agencies that only use LinkedIn** | Wrong channel. They won't see value. |
| **Agencies with in-house dev team** | Will want to build internally. |
| **Agencies focused on C-level / executive search** | Low-volume, relationship-based. Don't use FB groups. |
| **Agencies that just launched (< 6 months)** | No established process to improve. No budget. |
| **Anyone who asks for API / white-label / custom integrations** | Scope creep. Not pilot material. |
| **Agencies outside Israel** | Different market, different groups, different language. Not wave 1. |

## Qualifying Questions (Quick Check)

Before investing time in outreach, answer these:

| Question | Good Answer | Bad Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Do they post to Facebook groups? | Yes, regularly | No / only LinkedIn |
| How many groups? | 10+ | "A few" / 1–2 |
| How many recruiters? | 3–15 | 1 or 50+ |
| Can I find the owner's WhatsApp? | Yes, or within 1 search | Unreachable |
| Are they in tech / blue collar / service? | Yes | Executive search / niche |

**If 4/5 are "Good" → outreach. If 3/5 → maybe. If <3 → skip.**
