{{ ui("faq_title") }}

How does the AI screening work?

Candidates click "Apply" on your job posting in Telegram. The bot conducts a short interview (3-5 questions), evaluates answers with AI, and gives each candidate a score. You only see qualified candidates.

What languages are supported?

Russian, Hebrew, and English. The bot auto-detects the candidate's language from their Telegram settings.

Can I try it for free?

Yes! Free 2-week trial. Create one vacancy, post to a Telegram group, and see candidates flowing in.

Do I need technical skills?

No. Everything is managed through a web dashboard. Set up a vacancy, write screening questions, and the AI handles the rest.
