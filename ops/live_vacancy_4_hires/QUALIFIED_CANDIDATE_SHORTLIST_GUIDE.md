# Qualified Candidate Shortlist Guide

Use `qualified_candidates_shortlist.csv` for the recruiter-facing shortlist.

## When to add a candidate
- after the candidate is clearly `fit`
- after minimum screening is complete
- before or during recruiter handoff

## Shortlist rule
- one row per candidate
- keep the summary short
- keep the next step concrete
- if fit is still unclear, keep the candidate in `candidate_pipeline.csv`, not here

## Minimum information
- candidate name
- contact
- source
- location
- summary of fit
- key risk if any
- next step
