Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Posting Autopilot ("we", "the service") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the service you agree to this policy.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: your email, company/business name, and password (stored only as a salted hash — never in plain text).
  • Billing data: handled by our payment processor (Stripe). We store only a customer/subscription identifier and your plan — never your full card number.
  • Connected-channel credentials: Telegram session data and Facebook access tokens you connect, stored to operate posting on your behalf.
  • Listing & campaign content you create.
  • Lead data: names, phone numbers, and messages of people who respond to your listings, collected on your behalf so you can follow up.
  • Prospect data used for outreach (business name, public contact email/phone).

2. How We Use Data

We use this data solely to operate the service for you: publishing your listings, running the AI assistant that answers and qualifies responders, delivering leads to your dashboard, processing billing, and sending you notifications. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Processors

We share the minimum necessary data with: Stripe (billing), OpenAI (AI screening of responses), Telegram and Facebook (to post and receive messages on connected accounts), and our transactional email provider. Each processes data only to provide its function.

4. Marketing / Outreach Email

Outreach emails we send on your behalf include the sender's identity, a physical postal address, and a one-click unsubscribe link, in compliance with the US CAN-SPAM Act. Anyone may opt out at any time using that link, and we honor opt-outs promptly.

5. Data Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. After cancellation, data is retained for 30 days to allow recovery, then deleted. You may request earlier deletion (see Your Rights).

6. Your Rights

You can access, export, correct, or delete your data, and withdraw consent, at any time by contacting us through the dashboard. We honor applicable rights under the GDPR (EU/EEA) and CCPA (California), including the right to know, delete, and opt out of any sale of personal information (we do not sell it).

7. Security

Connected-channel credentials are stored with restricted access, passwords are salted-and-hashed, and billing card data never touches our servers. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

8. Children

The service is for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us through the platform dashboard.
