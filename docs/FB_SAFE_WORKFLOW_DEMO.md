# Facebook Safe Workflow — Demo & Pitch Narrative

---

## 5-Minute Demo Script

### Setup (30 seconds)
> "Let me show you how Recruit Autopilot handles Facebook group posting — safely, fast, and with full tracking."

Open the dashboard. Show the vacancy list.

---

### Scene 1: Vacancy → AI Post (60 seconds)

1. Click on a vacancy: **"Senior Java Developer — Tel Aviv — 35K NIS"**
2. Show the vacancy details (title, requirements, location, salary range, benefits)
3. Click **"Generate Facebook Post"**
4. AI generates a Hebrew-language post in 3 seconds:
   - Attention-grabbing opener
   - Key details (role, location, salary hint, top benefits)
   - Call to action ("Send CV to DM" or "Comment 'interested'")
   - Relevant emoji, clean formatting
5. Show the **tone selector**: Professional / Casual / Urgent / Young-audience
6. Switch to "Casual" — post regenerates with different language
7. Click **"Approve"** — post is ready

> "That's it. No writing, no templates, no copy-paste from Word. AI writes it, you approve it."

---

### Scene 2: Group Selection (60 seconds)

1. Click **"Select Groups"**
2. Show the **Group Directory**:
   - Groups organized by: category (Tech / Finance / Blue Collar / General), city, size, activity level
   - Each group shows: name, member count, activity rating (1–5), last posted date, posting rules summary
3. Filter: **Category = Tech, City = Tel Aviv, Activity = 4+**
4. Result: 12 groups shown
5. Check 8 groups with one click on **"Select Top Groups"**
6. Show that 2 groups are flagged: "Last posted < 24h ago" — system warns against double-posting

> "You know exactly where you're posting, which groups are active, and when you last posted there."

---

### Scene 3: Posting Queue (90 seconds)

1. Click **"Start Posting"**
2. Screen shows the **Posting Queue**:
   - Left: the approved post (with copy button)
   - Right: list of selected groups with direct links
3. Click **"Copy Post"** — post is on clipboard
4. Click the first group link — Facebook opens in a new tab, directly on the group
5. Paste the post, click Facebook's "Post" button
6. Come back to Recruit Autopilot
7. Click **"Mark as Posted"** on that group — it turns green with timestamp
8. Next group is highlighted automatically
9. Show the **"Open Next Group"** button — one click to move forward

> "Copy, click, paste, post, mark. Five seconds per group. No browser extension, no bot, no risk."

**Key moment:** show the counter — "8 groups, estimated time: 2 minutes"

---

### Scene 4: Results Tracking (60 seconds)

1. Go to **"Results"** view
2. Show a table: Vacancy × Group × Post date × Status
3. Statuses: Posted / Got responses / Got CVs / Interview scheduled / Hired
4. Click on one group — show detail:
   - Post text
   - Posted at: March 28, 14:32
   - Responses: 7 comments, 3 DMs
   - CVs received: 4
   - Quality rating: 3/5 (recruiter-rated)
5. Show the **Group Performance** chart:
   - "Tech Jobs Tel Aviv" → 12 CVs from last 5 posts
   - "Hi-Tech Israel" → 3 CVs from last 5 posts

> "Now you know which groups actually work. Stop posting everywhere — post where it matters."

---

### Close (30 seconds)
> "That's Recruit Autopilot for Facebook. AI writes the posts. You choose the groups. You post with one click. Everything is tracked. And you'll never get banned, because there's no automation touching Facebook. Want to try it for 14 days, free?"

## Objection Handling

### "We already use an autoposter and it works fine"
> "It works until it doesn't. Facebook runs enforcement waves — when they catch your tool, you lose everything at once. We've seen agencies lose pages, groups, and ad accounts in a single day. Our approach gives you the same speed without the risk. And honestly — our tracking alone is worth the switch, because you probably don't know which groups actually produce hires."

### "Manual posting is too slow, even with your tool"
> "Let me show you the numbers. With our tool, each group takes about 15 seconds — copy, click, paste, post, mark. 20 groups = 5 minutes. Without us, 20 groups = 40 minutes of writing, finding groups, and keeping track. We're not asking you to go slow. We're making manual posting fast enough that automation isn't worth the risk."

### "We tried Facebook groups and they don't work for us"
> "Which groups did you try? Most agencies post to the same 10 popular groups. Our directory has 500+ categorized groups with activity ratings. The difference between a dead group and an active one is 10x in response rate. And with our tracking, you'll know within a week which groups work for your specific vacancy types."

### "This is too expensive for what it does"
> "A single Facebook account ban costs you your page, your groups, your ad account, and weeks of rebuilding. That's thousands in lost productivity and marketing spend. Our Professional plan is 999 NIS/month — less than one day of a recruiter's salary. And the tracking data alone pays for itself by eliminating wasted posting to dead groups."

### "We'll just build something internal"
> "You could. But you'd need to build the AI content generation in Hebrew (which is harder than English), the group directory with ongoing curation, the posting queue UX, and the tracking pipeline. That's 3–6 months of dev time. We're ready now, and you can try it in 15 minutes."

### "We only use LinkedIn / job boards"
> "Facebook groups reach candidates who never open LinkedIn or AllJobs — especially blue-collar, service industry, and junior tech roles. It's not a replacement for what you do — it's an additional channel that your competitors are already using. The question isn't whether Facebook works. It's whether you're leaving candidates on the table."
