# First Pilot Sourcing Playbook

---

## Overview

Goal: find 20–30 qualified prospects in 2–3 hours of manual research.

Work sources in this order — highest signal first:

```
1. Facebook Groups (best — direct evidence of posting behavior)
2. LinkedIn (good — find agencies and contacts)
3. Google Search (good — find agencies you missed)
4. Job Boards (moderate — find active agencies)
5. Telegram (moderate — find recruiters in channels)
6. Business Directories (low — backfill)
```

---

## Source 1: Facebook Groups

### Why This Is the Best Source
You see the prospect doing the exact thing your product helps with. You get their name, agency, posting style, and frequency — all in one place.

### What to Do

**Step 1: Join 10–15 high-traffic Israeli job groups**

Search Facebook for:
```
דרושים תל אביב
דרושים הייטק
דרושים ישראל
עבודה בהייטק
משרות הייטק
דרושים חיפה
דרושים באר שבע
דרושים עובדים
עבודה בישראל
Tech Jobs Israel
```

Join the top 10–15 groups by member count. Focus on groups with 10K+ members.

**Step 2: Scroll and Identify Agency Posters**

In each group, scroll through the last 2–3 days of posts. Look for:

| Look For | Why |
|---|---|
| Posts that mention an agency name | Direct lead |
| Posts from profiles that say "recruiter at [agency]" | Direct lead |
| Same person posting multiple vacancies | High-volume poster = high pain |
| Identical text posted by same person in multiple groups | Manual copy-paste = our ICP |
| Posts with bad formatting / plain text | Will value AI-generated posts |
| Posts with "URGENT" / "HOT" / "ASAP" | High-volume agency, acute need |

**Step 3: Capture the Lead**

For each agency poster you find, record:
- Poster's name
- Agency name (from post or profile)
- Facebook profile link
- Group where you found them
- What the post looked like (screenshot optional)
- Estimated volume (how many posts in last 3 days?)

**Step 4: Quick Qualify**

Check their Facebook profile:
- Is it a personal account posting for an agency? → Good
- Is it an agency page? → Find the person behind it
- Do they post in 3+ groups? → Tier 1
- Do they post the same text everywhere? → Tier 1

**Time: 15–20 minutes per group. 10 groups = 2.5–3 hours. Expected yield: 15–25 leads.**

### Signals That Matter

| Signal | Tier | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Same post in 5+ groups, copy-pasted | Tier 1 | Maximum pain, manual process |
| Posts multiple vacancies per day | Tier 1 | High volume, time pressure |
| Posts in both Hebrew and English | Tier 1 | Sophisticated, cares about reach |
| Uses emoji/formatting but still manual | Tier 1 | Cares about quality, will love AI |
| One post, one group | Tier 3 | Low volume, lower pain |
| Post looks auto-generated (weird formatting) | Tier 1 | May be using autoposter — switch pitch |

## Source 2: LinkedIn

### What to Search

**Search 1: People**
```
Query: "recruiter" OR "מגייס" OR "talent acquisition"
Location: Israel
```

**Search 2: Companies**
```
Query: "recruitment agency" OR "staffing" OR "כח אדם" OR "השמה"
Location: Israel
```

**Search 3: Posts**
```
Query: "Facebook groups" OR "פייסבוק" AND "hiring" OR "recruiting"
```

### What to Look For

| Signal | Tier | Capture |
|---|---|---|
| Agency page with 5–30 employees | Tier 1–2 | Agency name, size, sector |
| Recruiter who mentions FB in their posts | Tier 1 | Name, agency, LinkedIn URL |
| Job posts mentioning "send CV on Facebook" | Tier 2 | Agency name |
| Agency with many open roles on LinkedIn | Tier 2 | Likely also posting on FB |
| Recruiter complaining about Facebook reach | Tier 1 | Screenshot, name, agency |

### What to Capture
- Agency name
- LinkedIn company page URL
- Number of employees (from page)
- Key contact (founder, CEO, ops manager)
- Their LinkedIn profile URL
- Whether they mention Facebook anywhere

**Time: 30–45 minutes. Expected yield: 10–15 leads.**

## Source 3: Google Search

### Search Queries (Hebrew)

```
"סוכנות גיוס" + "תל אביב"
"חברת השמה" + "ישראל"
"שירותי גיוס" + "הייטק"
"כח אדם" + "גיוס"
"recruitment agency" + "Israel"
"staffing agency" + "Tel Aviv"
```

### Search Queries (Google Maps)
```
Search: "סוכנות גיוס" or "recruitment agency"
Location: Tel Aviv (then repeat for Haifa, Jerusalem, Beer Sheva)
```

### What to Look For
- Agency website → About page → team size
- Agency website → mentions Facebook as a channel
- Agency website → contact page → phone / WhatsApp / email
- Google reviews → indicates active business
- Google Maps listing → phone number

### What to Capture
- Agency name
- Website URL
- Phone number from website/Google
- Estimated size (from team page or LinkedIn cross-reference)
- Whether they mention Facebook on their site
- Contact name if visible

**Time: 20–30 minutes. Expected yield: 5–10 leads.**

## Source 4: Job Boards (AllJobs, Drushim, JobMaster)

### What to Do

Go to:
- alljobs.co.il
- drushim.co.il
- jobmaster.co.il

**Search for agencies posting high volumes:**
- Sort by "most recent" in tech / service / blue collar categories
- Look for agency names that appear repeatedly
- Note: agencies posting 10+ vacancies on job boards are likely also on Facebook

### What to Capture
- Agency name
- Sector focus
- Volume of postings (rough count)
- Contact info if listed

### Cross-Reference
For each agency found here, search Facebook for their name → check if they also post in groups.

**Time: 15–20 minutes. Expected yield: 5–8 leads.**

## Source 5: Telegram

### Where to Look

Search Telegram for:
```
HR Israel
גיוס עובדים
משאבי אנוש
recruiters Israel
דרושים
```

Join 3–5 active channels/groups.

### What to Look For
- Recruiters sharing job posts (same behavior as Facebook → same pain)
- Recruiters asking for tool recommendations
- Recruiters complaining about Facebook
- Agency names mentioned in discussions

### What to Capture
- Recruiter name + Telegram handle
- Agency name
- Any mention of Facebook posting behavior

**Time: 15 minutes. Expected yield: 3–5 leads.**

---

## Source 6: Israeli Business Directories

### Directories to Check
- d.co.il — Israeli yellow pages → search "גיוס" or "השמה"
- zap.co.il/business — search "recruitment"
- b144.co.il — search "כח אדם"

### What to Capture
- Agency name
- Phone number
- Address/city
- Website (if listed)

### When to Use
Only as backfill if you haven't hit 20 leads from sources 1–5. Low signal — you don't know if they use Facebook.

**Time: 10 minutes. Expected yield: 3–5 leads.**

## Sourcing Session Structure

### The 3-Hour Sourcing Sprint

| Block | Source | Time | Expected Leads |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facebook Groups (5 groups) | 60 min | 10–12 |
| 2 | LinkedIn search | 30 min | 6–8 |
| 3 | Google search | 20 min | 4–5 |
| 4 | Facebook Groups (3 more groups) | 40 min | 5–8 |
| 5 | Job boards / Telegram / directories | 20 min | 3–5 |
| 6 | De-duplicate, score, prioritize | 10 min | — |
| **Total** | | **3 hours** | **25–35 raw leads** |

After de-duplication and qualification: **20–25 usable leads.**

### Output
At the end of the sprint, your lead sheet should have:
- 8–10 Tier 1 leads (confirmed FB group posters)
- 8–10 Tier 2 leads (likely FB users, need confirmation)
- 5–10 Tier 3 leads (backfill)

---

## Quality Over Quantity Rule

**Stop at 25–30 leads.** Don't build a list of 100. You're a founder doing personal outreach. 20–30 well-researched leads with personalized messages will outperform 100 spray-and-pray contacts.

For wave 1:
- 10 Tier 1 leads with WhatsApp = enough to get 2–3 pilots
- If wave 1 doesn't produce pilots, source 10 more and repeat
- Never source more than 1 week ahead
