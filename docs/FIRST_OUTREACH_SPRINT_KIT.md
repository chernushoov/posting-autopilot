## What to Capture Per Lead (Minimum Viable)

Copy this into a note or directly into your sheet as you go.

```
Agency: ___
Found in: [group name]
Poster name: ___
What I saw: [e.g., "same post in 4 groups, plain text, tech roles"]
Tier: 1 / 2 / 3
Phone visible: Yes / No
Profile link: ___
```

**That's it. Do not research more during sourcing. Sourcing and contact research are separate steps.**

---

## "Good Enough" Rules

### Good Enough to Add to Sheet
- You can see their agency name OR they're clearly a recruiter posting for a company
- They posted in at least 1 Facebook group
- You captured their name and a profile link

### NOT Good Enough — Skip
- Random person posting a single "looking for work" message
- Page/brand account with no identifiable person behind it (unless you can find the person in 30 seconds)
- Post is from 30+ days ago (they may have stopped)

---

## Hard Stop Rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Do not spend more than 5 minutes on any single lead during sourcing** | Sourcing is volume. Research is separate. |
| **Do not start messaging during the sourcing phase** | Finish your list first. Message in batch. |
| **Do not try to find phone numbers during sourcing** | That's the research step. Separate. |
| **Stop sourcing when you have 25 raw leads** | Enough for wave 1. More = diminishing returns. |
| **If a group has no agency posters in last 3 days, leave and move on** | Dead group. Don't scroll forever. |
| **If you've been in a group for 10 minutes with <2 leads, move on** | Time-box per group. |

## Batch Workflow: First 10 Leads in 45 Minutes

This is the fastest path to your first 10 leads.

### Minute 0–5: Setup
- Open Facebook. Search `דרושים הייטק`. Join top 3 groups.
- Open your lead sheet (or a blank doc).

### Minute 5–20: Scroll Group 1
- Scroll the last 2–3 days of posts.
- For every recruiter/agency post: capture agency name, poster name, profile link, what you saw.
- Target: 3–5 leads.

### Minute 20–35: Scroll Group 2
- Same process. Different group from the same search.
- Target: 2–3 leads.

### Minute 35–45: Scroll Group 3 (or switch to query #2)
- If group 2 was thin, switch to `דרושים תל אביב` groups.
- Target: 2–3 leads.

**Minute 45: You should have 7–11 raw leads. If yes, move to contact research for the top 5. If no, do one more group.**

---

## After Sourcing: Contact Research Batch (30 min)

Take your top 10 leads (highest tier first). For each:

| Step | Time | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30 sec | Open their Facebook profile. Check "About" for phone. |
| 2 | 30 sec | Open their agency's Facebook page. Check "About" for phone. |
| 3 | 30 sec | Google "[agency name] טלפון" — check first 3 results. |
| 4 | 30 sec | If still no phone: LinkedIn search "[person name] [agency]" → note URL. |
| 5 | — | If no phone in 2 minutes, mark "No Phone" and move on. |

**Expected result: 6–8 out of 10 will have a phone number. That's enough to start messaging.**
