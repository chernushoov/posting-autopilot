## 5-Minute Call Opener

Use: scheduled discovery/demo call. First 5 minutes before showing anything.

### Opening (30 sec)
> "Thanks for taking the time. I'll keep this short — I want to understand how you're currently handling Facebook posting, and then I'll show you what we built. Sound good?"

### Context Set (60 sec)
> "Quick background: I've been talking to recruitment agencies across Israel. Almost everyone posts to Facebook groups — it's one of the best channels for finding candidates, especially for tech, blue-collar, and service roles.
>
> But the process is painful. Either you spend hours copy-pasting, or you use an autoposter and risk getting banned. Both options suck.
>
> We built a third option. But before I show you, I want to understand your setup."

### Discovery (2–3 min)
Move to discovery questions below. Listen. Take notes. Tailor the demo based on what they say.

### Bridge to Demo (30 sec)
> "Got it. So you're posting to [X] groups, spending about [Y] hours, and [tracking/not tracking] results. Let me show you how our tool changes that — I'll use a real vacancy as an example."

## Discovery Call Structure

**Total time: 15–20 minutes**

### Phase 1: Current State (5 min)

Ask in order. Stop when you have enough to tailor the demo.

1. **"How many vacancies do you typically post to Facebook groups per week?"**
   - Listen for: volume (5? 20? 50?). High volume = bigger pain.

2. **"How many Facebook groups are you posting to?"**
   - Listen for: 10 groups = light user. 30+ = power user. 80+ = desperate for a solution.

3. **"Who does the posting — each recruiter, or one dedicated person?"**
   - Listen for: if one person does it, that person is your champion. If each recruiter does it, the pain is distributed.

4. **"How long does it take per day?"**
   - Listen for: anything over 1 hour = strong pain. Write down the exact number they say — use it later.

5. **"How do you track which groups you've posted to and which ones get results?"**
   - Listen for: "we don't" or "Excel" or "in my head" = massive opportunity.

### Phase 2: Pain & Risk (3 min)

6. **"Have you or anyone at your agency ever been blocked or banned by Facebook?"**
   - If yes: dig in. What happened? What did they lose? This is your strongest lever.
   - If no: "You've been lucky. I've talked to agencies that lost 3 pages and 12 groups in one week from an autoposter."

7. **"Are you using any automation tools for Facebook posting?"**
   - If yes: "Which one? How long have you been using it?"
   - Don't attack their tool. Just note: "Good to know. I'll show you how we compare."
   - If no: "Smart. Most tools in this space violate Facebook's Terms."

### Phase 3: Bridge (2 min)

8. **"If you could change one thing about your Facebook posting process, what would it be?"**
   - Let them answer. Their words become your demo framing.

9. **"Let me show you how we solve [exactly what they said]. I'll use a real vacancy as an example."**

## Demo Call Structure

**Total time: 15–20 minutes (includes discovery if not done separately)**

### Pre-Demo Setup
- Have a real vacancy ready in the system (or a realistic demo one)
- Have the group directory loaded with 20+ groups
- Have sample results data for the tracking screen
- Screen share ready, browser open

### Demo Flow (10 min)

**Scene 1: AI Post Generation (2 min)**
1. "Here's a vacancy — [Job Title, City, Salary]. Watch this."
2. Click Generate. Post appears in 3 seconds.
3. "No writing. No templates. AI writes it in Hebrew with the right tone."
4. Show tone switch: Professional → Casual. Post regenerates.
5. "You approve it. That's it."

**Scene 2: Group Selection (2 min)**
1. "Now, where do we post? Here's your group directory."
2. Show filters: category, city, activity level.
3. "See these ratings? After a few weeks, you know which groups actually bring CVs."
4. Select 6–8 groups. Show the double-posting warning.
5. "No guessing. No spreadsheets."

**Scene 3: Posting Queue (3 min)**
1. "Here's the posting queue. Post on the left. Groups on the right."
2. Click Copy. Click Open Group. Show Facebook tab opening.
3. "Paste. Post. Come back. Mark done. Next group."
4. Mark 2–3 groups as done. Show the progress.
5. "8 groups in 2 minutes. No bot. No risk."

**Scene 4: Results (2 min)**
1. "This is where it gets interesting. Every post is tracked."
2. Show results table: group × CVs × quality.
3. "See this group? 12 CVs from 5 posts. This one? 2 CVs from 5 posts. Now you know where to focus."
4. "This data doesn't exist anywhere else. No autoposter gives you this."

### Close (1 min)
> "That's it. AI writes, you pick groups, you post in 15 seconds each, everything is tracked.
>
> We're running 14-day free pilots right now. No credit card. I'll set you up in 15 minutes, and we'll publish your first post together.
>
> Want to start this week?"

## Objection Handling — Founder Cheat Sheet

Keep responses under 30 seconds. Acknowledge, reframe, redirect.

### Price / Value

**"This is too expensive"**
> "One Facebook ban costs you your page, your groups, your ad account, and weeks of rebuilding. That's thousands. We're 999 a month — less than one day of a recruiter's salary. And the tracking data alone saves hours of wasted posting to dead groups."

**"We can build this internally"**
> "You could. Hebrew AI generation, group curation, posting UX, tracking pipeline — that's 3–6 months of dev time. We're ready in 15 minutes. Try the pilot first — if it doesn't work, you've lost nothing."

**"We're not ready to pay for another tool"**
> "That's why the pilot is free. 14 days, no card. If it doesn't save time and produce results, you walk away. No cost, no risk."

### Competition / Alternatives

**"We already use an autoposter"**
> "Which one? [Listen.] Here's the thing — it works until Facebook catches it. When they do, you lose everything at once. We give you the same speed without the risk. Plus tracking that no autoposter has."

**"We just use copy-paste from a Word doc"**
> "That works, but it's slow and there's no tracking. Our AI generates better posts in 3 seconds, and you'll finally know which groups actually bring candidates."

**"Facebook groups don't work for us"**
> "Which groups did you try? Most agencies use the same 10 overcrowded groups. We have 200+ categorized groups with activity ratings. The right groups make a 10x difference."

**"We focus on LinkedIn / job boards"**
> "Facebook reaches candidates who never open LinkedIn — especially junior, blue-collar, and service roles. It's not a replacement. It's the channel your competitors are using that you're not."

### Timing / Urgency

**"Not a priority right now"**
> "Fair enough. Quick question — how many vacancies do you have open right now? [Number.] Each one that's not on Facebook is missing passive candidates. The pilot takes 15 minutes to set up. Worth trying?"

**"Maybe next quarter"**
> "I hear you. One thing to consider — we're only taking 20 early adopters at the pilot rate. After that, it's full price. If there's any chance you'll want this, locking in now makes sense."

**"Send me info and I'll look at it later"**
> "Happy to. But honestly, a 5-minute demo shows more than any doc. Can we do 5 minutes this week? If it's not relevant, I'll know in the first minute and won't waste your time."

### Trust / Skepticism

**"How do I know this actually works?"**
> "You'll see it in the pilot. First post goes out on Day 0. By Day 3, you'll have data. If it doesn't save time and produce CVs, you stop. No cost."

**"Is my data safe?"**
> "Yes. We don't access your Facebook account. We don't store passwords. We generate text and track results — that's it. The posting is always done by you, manually."

**"What if you shut down?"**
> "Your group directory and results data are exportable. No lock-in. And we're funded and growing — but you should never depend on any single tool, and we don't ask you to."

## Close / Next-Step Scripts

### After Discovery — They're Interested
> "Great. Here's what I suggest: I'll set up your account, load your groups, and we'll publish your first post together — takes about 15 minutes. When works this week?"

### After Demo — Ready to Start
> "Let's get you started. I need three things: your name, email, and a list of Facebook groups you post to. I'll set everything up and send you a link. We can do the first post together or you can start solo — your call."

### After Demo — Needs to Think
> "No problem. I'll send you a quick summary on WhatsApp. One thing — the early adopter pilot is limited to 20 agencies. If you decide to try it, just ping me and I'll set you up in 15 minutes."

### After Demo — Needs Boss Approval
> "Makes sense. Would it help if I sent a one-page summary you can forward? Or I can do a 5-minute call with your [boss/CEO/manager] — happy to jump on whenever works."

### After Demo — Says No
> "Totally fine. Can I ask — what's the main reason? [Listen.] Got it. If anything changes, the offer stands. One last thing — know any other agencies that post to Facebook groups? I'd appreciate an intro."

### Stalled Deal — Been 7+ Days
> "Hey [name], just checking in. The pilot offer is still open but I'm filling the early adopter spots. If you want to lock in the free trial + 50% first month, this week is the time. No pressure — just don't want you to miss it."
