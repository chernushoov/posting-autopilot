# Demo Operator Script

What the founder says while the customer tests. Keep it natural. Don't over-explain.

---

## Before They Start (30 sec)

> "I'm going to walk you through 6 steps. The whole thing takes about 10 minutes. I want to see where it's smooth and where it's not — so just do what feels natural and tell me if anything is confusing. There are no wrong answers."

---

## Step 1: Enter Vacancy

**Say:** "Start by entering a vacancy you're actually working on right now."

**Watch for:** Do they know where to type? Do the fields make sense? Do they hesitate on anything?

**If they struggle:** point them to the right field, but note what confused them.

**Don't say:** Don't explain what every field does upfront. Let them try.

---

## Step 2: Generate Post

**Say:** "Now click Generate. Let's see what the AI writes."

**Watch for:** Their reaction to the output. Do they read it? Do they smile? Frown? Start editing immediately?

**After it generates, ask:** "What do you think? Would you post this?"

**If they want to edit:** "Go ahead — change whatever you want."

**If they like it:** "Try switching the tone — hit Casual or Urgent and see what changes."

**Don't say:** Don't defend the AI output. If they don't like it, ask: "What would you change?"

---

## Step 3: Pick Groups

**Say:** "Now pick a few groups to post to. Use the filters if you want."

**Watch for:** Do they find groups they recognize? Do the filters work? Do they try to add their own groups?

**If the directory feels thin:** "We're still building the full directory — if your groups aren't here, we'll add them."

**Don't say:** Don't apologize for missing groups. Just note which ones they expected.

---

## Step 4: Post to Groups

**Say:** "This is the posting flow. Copy the post, open the group, paste, post, come back and mark it done."

**Watch for:** Does copy-to-clipboard work? Does the group link open correctly? Can they find their way back? Do they mark it done?

**After the first group:** "How was that? How long does this normally take you?"

**After 2–3 groups:** "You can keep going or stop here — up to you."

**Don't say:** Don't rush them. Don't do it for them. Let them feel the flow.

---

## Step 5: Track Results

**Say:** "Last thing — this is where you track what happened. You can update it later with how many responses or CVs came in."

**Watch for:** Do they understand the tracking? Does it feel useful or like extra work?

**Ask:** "Do you currently track which groups give you results?" (The answer is almost always no — which proves the value.)

---

## Step 6: Feedback

**Say:** "That's it. Three questions:"

> 1. "What worked well?"
> 2. "What was annoying or confusing?"
> 3. "Would you use this every day?"

**Listen. Don't defend. Write down exactly what they say.**

**If they're positive:** "What would make it a must-have instead of a nice-to-have?"

**If they're negative:** "What would need to change for you to try it again?"

**Close:** "Thanks — this is really helpful. I'll fix what we talked about. Can I check in with you in a couple of days to show the improvements?"

---

## Rules for the Founder During the Test

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Watch their screen (screen share or in person) | Don't take control of their mouse/keyboard |
| Note where they hesitate | Don't explain things before they try |
| Ask "what did you expect?" when confused | Don't say "that's a known issue" |
| Write down their exact words | Don't argue with their feedback |
| Stay quiet during the flow — let them drive | Don't fill silence with explanations |
| Thank them at the end | Don't pitch the paid plan during the test |
