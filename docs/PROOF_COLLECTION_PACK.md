## Before/After Metrics Template

Collect "before" during onboarding call. Collect "after" at pilot review.

### Data Collection Script

**During Onboarding (Before):**
> "Before we start — I want to capture your baseline so we can compare later.
>
> 1. How many minutes per day do you spend posting to Facebook groups? [___]
> 2. How many groups do you post to per vacancy? [___]
> 3. How many CVs per week do you get from Facebook groups? [___]
> 4. Do you know which groups produce the best candidates? [Yes/No]
> 5. Have you ever been banned or restricted by Facebook? [Yes/No/Not sure]"

**At Pilot Review (After):**
> "Let's compare to where you started:
>
> 1. How many minutes per day do you spend posting now? [___]
> 2. How many groups are you posting to per vacancy now? [___]
> 3. How many CVs per week are you getting from Facebook groups now? [___]
> 4. Do you now know which groups produce the best candidates? [Yes/No]
> 5. Anything else that changed?"

### Before/After Comparison Card

Use this format for testimonials, case studies, and marketing.

```
[Agency Name] — Before & After

BEFORE Recruit Autopilot:
⏱ [X] minutes/day posting to Facebook
📋 [Y] groups per vacancy
📩 [Z] CVs/week from Facebook
🔍 No visibility into which groups work
⚠️ [Ban history if any]

AFTER 14 Days:
⏱ [X'] minutes/day (↓ [N]%)
📋 [Y'] groups per vacancy (↑ [N]%)
📩 [Z'] CVs/week (↑ [N]%)
🔍 Knows top 5 performing groups
✅ Zero ban risk
```

## Case Study Interview Questions

**Duration:** 15 minutes. Record (with permission) or take notes.

### Background (2 min)
1. Tell me about your agency — how many recruiters, what sectors?
2. How do you typically source candidates?

### Before the Tool (3 min)
3. How did you handle Facebook group posting before Recruit Autopilot?
4. How much time per day did it take?
5. Did you use any automation tools? What happened?
6. How did you decide which groups to post to?
7. How did you track results?

### The Switch (2 min)
8. What made you decide to try Recruit Autopilot?
9. How was the onboarding experience?

### Results (5 min)
10. How has your posting process changed?
11. How much time do you save per day/week?
12. Have you seen a difference in the number or quality of CVs?
13. Which feature do you use the most?
14. Have you discovered new groups through the directory?
15. Do you now know which groups work best for which roles?

### Overall (3 min)
16. What surprised you most?
17. What would you improve?
18. Would you recommend it to other agencies? Why?
19. Can I quote you? (Full name, or anonymous?)

### Case Study Output Format
```
# Case Study: [Agency Name]

## The Challenge
[2–3 sentences about their situation before the tool]

## The Solution
[2–3 sentences about what they did with Recruit Autopilot]

## The Results
- [Metric 1: time saved]
- [Metric 2: groups expanded]
- [Metric 3: CVs increased]
- [Metric 4: group intelligence gained]

## Quote
"[Direct quote from client]"
— [Name], [Title], [Agency]
```

## Proof Points Checklist

Track which proof points you've collected. These are the building blocks for all marketing.

### Quantitative Proof

- [ ] Time saved: "[X] minutes saved per day" — from at least 3 clients
- [ ] Volume increase: "Posting to [X]% more groups" — from at least 2 clients
- [ ] CV increase: "[X] more CVs per week from Facebook" — from at least 2 clients
- [ ] Group discovery: "Found [X] new high-performing groups" — from at least 1 client
- [ ] Speed metric: "[X] seconds per group" — timed and verified
- [ ] Ban avoidance: "[X] months using the tool with zero bans" — from at least 3 clients

### Qualitative Proof

- [ ] Testimonial quote (attributed) — at least 2
- [ ] Testimonial quote (anonymous) — at least 2
- [ ] "Would recommend" confirmation — at least 5
- [ ] Specific use case story (e.g., "filled a position through a group I'd never heard of") — at least 1
- [ ] Comparison quote ("better than [competitor/old process] because...") — at least 1

### Visual Proof

- [ ] Screenshot: AI-generated post example (Hebrew)
- [ ] Screenshot: group directory with ratings
- [ ] Screenshot: posting queue in progress
- [ ] Screenshot: results dashboard with real data (anonymized)
- [ ] Before/after comparison card (at least 1 client)
- [ ] Video: 60-second screen recording of full posting flow
