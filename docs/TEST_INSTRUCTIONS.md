# Customer Test Instructions

---

## What You're Testing

A tool that helps you post vacancies to Facebook groups faster, with AI-generated posts and tracking.

## What You Need

- A computer with a browser
- Your Facebook account (logged in)
- One open vacancy (title, location, requirements, salary)

---

## 6 Steps

### Step 1: Enter a Vacancy
Open the tool. Enter your vacancy details:
- Job title
- Location
- 3–5 requirements
- Salary range
- How to apply (DM / link / email)

### Step 2: Generate a Post
Click "Generate Post." The AI will write a Hebrew Facebook post in a few seconds.
- Read it
- Try switching the tone (Professional / Casual / Urgent)
- Edit if you want
- Click "Approve"

### Step 3: Pick Groups
Select Facebook groups to post to from the directory.
- Use filters (city, category) if available
- Pick 3–5 groups

### Step 4: Post to Groups
The posting queue shows your approved post and the group list.
- Click "Copy Post" — the text goes to your clipboard
- Click the group link — Facebook opens in a new tab
- Paste the post in the group and click Post on Facebook
- Come back and click "Done" to mark it
- Repeat for each group

### Step 5: Track Results
After posting, go to the Results screen.
- You should see each group you posted to
- Tomorrow: come back and update how many responses/CVs you got

### Step 6: Tell Us What Happened
After finishing all groups:
- What worked?
- What was confusing?
- What broke?
- Would you use this daily?

Use the bug report template if something didn't work right.

---

## Time Estimate

The whole test should take 10–15 minutes.
