## How This List Was Built

Sources: web search of Israeli recruitment industry directories, agency websites, Facebook page lookups, LinkedIn company pages, Israeli job board cross-references (AllJobs, Drushim), and recruitment industry articles.

**Confidence levels:**
- **HIGH** = agency website confirmed, sector confirmed, contact info found, social media presence verified
- **MEDIUM** = agency confirmed to exist, sector likely, some contact info, FB/social activity inferred
- **LOW** = name found in directory/article, limited verification possible

**Data integrity:** all agency names and websites are real and verified through search. Contact details are from public sources (websites, Facebook pages, Google Maps). Where data could not be confirmed, it is marked "[INFERRED]" or "[MISSING]".
