# First Pilot Targeting Model

---

## Tier System

### Tier 1 — High Priority (Contact First)

**Profile:** Agency that is visibly posting to Facebook groups right now, manual process, 3–15 recruiters, reachable owner.

**Signals:**
- You found their posts in Facebook groups today/this week
- Posts are copy-paste (identical text across groups)
- Posting to 5+ groups
- Agency name visible in posts or profile
- Owner/founder findable on LinkedIn or Facebook

**Expected conversion:** 20–30% to pilot
**Expected friction:** Low. They feel the pain. Demo sells itself.
**Outreach approach:** WhatsApp first. Reference the specific group where you saw their post. Personalized.

**Target: 8–10 Tier 1 prospects in wave 1.**

---

### Tier 2 — Medium Priority (Contact Second)

**Profile:** Agency known to use Facebook groups, but you haven't seen their posts directly. OR agency that had a Facebook ban/restriction.

**Signals:**
- Agency website mentions Facebook as a channel
- Found via Google/LinkedIn, agency matches ICP size and sector
- Someone in a forum/group mentioned getting banned (or complained about FB posting)
- Active on job boards (AllJobs, Drushim) with high volume — likely also on FB
- Recruiter from this agency is active in Telegram HR groups

**Expected conversion:** 10–20% to pilot
**Expected friction:** Medium. Need to confirm they use FB groups. May need a call before demo.
**Outreach approach:** WhatsApp or email. Less personalized — ask if they post to FB groups first.

**Target: 8–10 Tier 2 prospects in wave 1.**

---

### Tier 3 — Opportunistic (Contact If Easy)

**Profile:** Agencies found through directories or referrals, but no confirmed FB group activity.

**Signals:**
- Found on Google Maps / business directory as "recruitment agency"
- Referred by someone but no details on their process
- Large agency that might have a dedicated posting person
- Agency that posts only to their own Facebook page (not groups)

**Expected conversion:** 5–10% to pilot
**Expected friction:** High. May not use FB groups. May not be interested. Longer qualification.
**Outreach approach:** Email or LinkedIn. Discovery-first — qualify before pitching.

**Target: 5–10 Tier 3 prospects as backfill.**

## Prioritization Matrix

| Factor | Weight | Tier 1 Score | Tier 2 Score | Tier 3 Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Confirmed FB group posting | 5 | 5 (visible) | 3 (likely) | 1 (unknown) |
| Agency size 3–15 | 3 | 3 (confirmed) | 2 (estimated) | 1 (unknown) |
| Owner reachable | 4 | 4 (found) | 2 (findable) | 1 (unknown) |
| Pain signal (ban/complaint/chaos) | 3 | 3 (observed) | 2 (inferred) | 0 |
| Sector fit (tech/blue/service) | 2 | 2 (confirmed) | 1 (likely) | 1 (unknown) |
| **Total** | | **17** | **10** | **4** |

**Rule: work Tier 1 list until exhausted before moving to Tier 2.**

## Outreach Approach by Tier

### Tier 1: Personalized WhatsApp

```
היי [שם],

ראיתי את הפוסטים שלכם בקבוצת [שם קבוצה ספציפי].

בניתי כלי שעושה את זה 5x יותר מהר — AI כותב, ספריית קבוצות חכמה, מעקב מלא. בלי סיכון חסימה.

יש דמו של 5 דקות — מעניין?
```

**Key: mention the specific group. Proves you're real, not spam.**

### Tier 2: Qualifying WhatsApp

```
היי [שם],

אני רואה שאתם מגייסים ב[תחום/עיר].

שאלה מהירה — מפרסמים משרות בקבוצות פייסבוק?

אם כן — בניתי כלי שחותך את הזמן ב-80%. אשמח להראות.
אם לא — סליחה על ההפרעה 😊
```

**Key: qualify first. Don't pitch if they don't use FB groups.**

### Tier 3: Discovery Email

```
Subject: מפרסמים משרות בפייסבוק?

שלום [שם],

אני בונה כלי למגייסים שמפרסמים בקבוצות פייסבוק. שאלה קצרה — האם זה חלק מהתהליך שלכם?

אם כן, אשמח ל-5 דקות. יש לי דמו קצר שמראה איך לעשות את זה 5x יותר מהר.

[שם]
```

**Key: just qualify. If they respond "yes" → escalate to Tier 2 approach.**

## Conversion Funnel Expectations (Wave 1)

```
Tier 1: 10 contacted → 5 respond → 3 demo → 2 pilot → 1 convert
Tier 2: 10 contacted → 3 respond → 2 demo → 1 pilot → 0.5 convert
Tier 3: 10 contacted → 2 respond → 1 demo → 0.5 pilot → 0.25 convert
```

**Wave 1 total (30 contacts): ~3–4 pilots, ~1–2 conversions**

This is realistic for founder-led outreach in Israel. One good pilot with data is enough to unlock the next 10.
