# First Pilot Contact Research Workflow

---

## Goal

For each agency on your lead sheet, find a reachable human with decision-making power. Get their WhatsApp number. Spend no more than 5 minutes per lead.

---

## Who to Find (In Priority Order)

| Role | Why | Decision Power |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner / Founder / CEO** | Makes decisions instantly. No approval needed. | ✅✅✅ |
| **Operations Manager** | Controls process. Can champion internally. | ✅✅ |
| **Lead Recruiter / Team Lead** | Feels the pain daily. Can champion upward. | ✅ |
| **The person who actually posts to Facebook** | May be any of the above, or a junior. Best entry point. | ✅ (for intro) |

**Ideal: the person who posts to Facebook groups IS the owner.** This is common in 3–10 person agencies.

---

## Contact Research Sequence

Work through these steps in order. Stop as soon as you have a WhatsApp number or phone number.

### Step 1: Facebook Profile (1 minute)

If you found this lead in a Facebook group:
1. Click on the poster's profile
2. Check their bio / "About" section
3. Look for: phone number, agency name, role
4. Check if their Facebook Messenger is open (fallback contact method)
5. Look at their profile for the agency they work at

**Capture:** Name, role, agency, phone (if visible)

### Step 2: Agency Facebook Page (1 minute)

Search Facebook for the agency name:
1. Find their Facebook business page
2. Check "About" section → phone number, WhatsApp, website
3. Check who manages the page (sometimes visible in "Page Transparency")
4. Check recent posts for contact info

**Capture:** Phone/WhatsApp from page, website URL

### Step 3: Agency Website (1 minute)

If you have their website:
1. Check "Contact" or "About" page
2. Look for: phone number, WhatsApp widget, email, team page
3. Team page → find owner/founder name
4. "About" page → sometimes has founder's story with name

**Capture:** Phone, email, founder name

### Step 4: LinkedIn (1 minute)

Search LinkedIn for:
1. The agency name → company page → employees
2. Filter employees by "Founder" / "CEO" / "Owner" / "Manager"
3. Check the founder's LinkedIn profile for contact info
4. If phone not visible → note LinkedIn URL for InMail or connection request

**Capture:** Contact name, role, LinkedIn URL, phone if visible

### Step 5: Google (1 minute)

Search Google for:
```
"[agency name]" + "טלפון"
"[agency name]" + "WhatsApp"
"[contact name]" + "[agency name]"
"[agency name]" + site:facebook.com
```

Also try:
- Google Maps listing → phone number
- d.co.il / b144.co.il → phone number

**Capture:** Phone, email, any contact info

## "Good Enough" Contact Standard

| Contact Quality | Definition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ **WhatsApp found** | Phone number of owner/ops manager | Outreach immediately |
| ✅ **Phone found** | Phone number, not confirmed WhatsApp | Try WhatsApp first, call if no response |
| ⚠️ **Email only** | Email of decision maker | Send email, note "no phone" |
| ⚠️ **LinkedIn only** | LinkedIn profile, no phone/email | Send connection request + message |
| ⚠️ **FB Messenger only** | Can reach via Facebook DM | Send message, but low response rate |
| ❌ **Agency phone only** | General company phone, no person | Call, ask for owner by name. Last resort. |
| ❌ **Nothing found** | Can't find any contact | Skip. Move to next lead. |

**"Good enough" = WhatsApp number of someone at the agency.** If you have that, stop researching and start outreaching.

---

## When to Stop Researching

### Per Lead: 5-Minute Rule
- Set a timer. If you don't have a contact in 5 minutes, mark the lead as "Researching — Hard" and move on.
- Come back to hard leads only after you've contacted all easy leads.

### Per Session: Diminishing Returns Rule
- If you have 10 leads with WhatsApp numbers → stop researching. Start outreaching.
- Research more leads only when your outreach pipeline runs dry (all contacted, waiting for responses).

### The 10-and-Go Rule
```
Sourced 25 leads?
├── 10+ have WhatsApp? → Stop. Outreach now.
├── 5-9 have WhatsApp? → Research 5 more. Then outreach.
└── <5 have WhatsApp? → Source more from Facebook groups.
```

## Contact Research Cheat Sheet

### Finding Phone Numbers in Israel

| Method | Likelihood |
|---|---|
| Facebook profile "About" section | Medium |
| Agency Facebook page "About" | High |
| Agency website contact page | High |
| Google Maps business listing | High |
| LinkedIn profile (premium shows more) | Low–Medium |
| d.co.il / b144.co.il search | Medium |
| Google: "[name]" + "[agency]" + "טלפון" | Medium |
| Ask in Facebook group (risky — don't do this) | ❌ Don't |

### Israeli Phone Number Format
- Mobile: 05X-XXX-XXXX (WhatsApp-compatible)
- Landline: 0X-XXX-XXXX (not WhatsApp)
- With country code: +972-5X-XXX-XXXX

**Always try to get a 05X number (mobile) — that's WhatsApp.**

### Verifying WhatsApp
1. Save the number in your phone
2. Open WhatsApp → new chat → search the number
3. If their profile appears → confirmed WhatsApp
4. If not → try calling

## Research Output Format

For each lead, your minimum viable contact record:

```
Agency: [name]
Contact: [name] — [role]
WhatsApp: [+972-XX-XXX-XXXX]
Source: [where you found them]
FB Activity: [confirmed / likely / unknown]
Pain Signal: [what you observed]
Tier: [1 / 2 / 3]
Ready to Contact: [Yes / No — if no, what's missing?]
```

When "Ready to Contact" = Yes for 10 leads → move to outreach.

## Batch Research Process

### For a List of 10 Leads

| Step | Action | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open all 10 Facebook profiles in tabs | 2 min |
| 2 | Scan each for phone/agency/role. Record what you find. | 5 min |
| 3 | For leads missing phone: open their agency websites in tabs | 2 min |
| 4 | Scan contact/about pages. Record phones. | 3 min |
| 5 | For remaining leads: quick LinkedIn search per agency | 5 min |
| 6 | For remaining leads: quick Google search | 3 min |
| 7 | Mark each lead: Ready / Need More / Skip | 2 min |
| **Total** | | **~22 min for 10 leads** |

**Expected result: 6–8 out of 10 will have a usable contact. 2–4 will need more work or should be skipped.**
