## "Why Now" Urgency Framing

Use these in calls, follow-ups, and close attempts. Pick the one that fits the prospect's situation.

### Framing 1: Early Adopter Scarcity
> "We're taking 20 agencies for the early adopter program. After that, it's full price with no pilot. You're number [X]. I want to make sure you get a spot before they're gone."

### Framing 2: Ban Risk Timing
> "Facebook runs enforcement waves — they come without warning. Every day you're using an autoposter is a day you could lose everything. The switch takes 15 minutes."

### Framing 3: Cost of Delay (Time)
> "Your recruiters spend 2–3 hours a day on Facebook posting. That's 10–15 hours a week. Every week you wait is another 15 hours of manual work you could cut by 80%."

### Framing 4: Cost of Delay (Data)
> "The sooner you start tracking, the sooner you know which groups work. Right now you're posting blind. In 14 days, you'll have real performance data on every group."

### Framing 5: Competitive
> "Your competitors are posting to the same groups. The ones who know which groups produce results — and which are a waste of time — will outperform. That data takes weeks to build. Start now."

## Scope Boundaries — What's In, What's Not

### What's Included in Pilot

| Included | Details |
|---|---|
| AI post generation | Unlimited, 4 tone options, Hebrew |
| Group directory | 200+ pre-loaded + your custom groups |
| Posting queue | Full workflow: copy, open, mark |
| Results tracking | Post → responses → CVs → hires |
| Onboarding | 15-min call, first post together |
| Support | WhatsApp during pilot |

### What's NOT Included (be upfront)

| Not Included | Why |
|---|---|
| Automatic posting to Facebook | Violates Facebook ToS. We never will. |
| Facebook API integration | Not available for group posting. |
| ATS/CRM integration | Coming in v2. Manual tracking for now. |
| Candidate management | We track group-level results, not individual candidates. |
| Multiple languages | Hebrew only in v1. English planned. |
| Mobile app | Web-based. Works on mobile browser but not optimized. |

### How to Handle "Can It Also Do X?"
> "Not yet — but that's exactly why we're running the early adopter program. If [X] is important to you, I'll add it to the roadmap. For now, the pilot focuses on posting speed, group organization, and results tracking. Let's nail those first."

## What Success Looks Like After 14 Days

### For the Client
Share this at the start of the pilot to set expectations:

> "In 14 days, here's what you'll have:
>
> 1. **20+ posts published** across 15+ groups — with zero manual writing
> 2. **A group directory** organized by city, category, and activity — no more guessing
> 3. **Results data** on every group: which ones got responses, which produced CVs
> 4. **Time savings** of 50–80% on your daily Facebook posting routine
> 5. **A clear picture** of whether Facebook groups are a real channel for your agency — backed by data, not gut feeling"

### For Internal Use (Founder's Success Criteria)

| Metric | Minimum for Conversion | Stretch |
|---|---|---|
| Posts published | 15 | 30+ |
| Unique groups used | 10 | 20+ |
| CVs received via tracked posts | 3 | 10+ |
| Time saved per day (self-reported) | 30 min | 1+ hour |
| Would recommend (1–5) | 4 | 5 |
| Ready to pay (yes/no) | Yes | Yes + referred someone |

### Red Flags During Pilot (Intervene Immediately)

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| No posts by Day 2 | Activation failed | Call. Do the first post together right now. |
| Only 1–2 groups used | Not exploring directory | Send curated group list for their vacancy type. |
| No login after Day 5 | Dropped off | WhatsApp: "Hey, everything OK? Need help?" |
| Uses tool but doesn't track results | Doesn't see tracking value | Show them sample insights: "Group X gave you 4 CVs. Group Y gave you 0." |
| Negative feedback on post quality | AI not calibrated | Offer to tune tone together. Ask what they'd change. |

## Pilot-to-Paid Conversion Script

### Day 12 — Pre-Close
```

מהנתונים שלנו:
📊 פרסמתם [X] משרות ב-[Y] קבוצות
📊 קיבלתם [Z] קו"ח
📊 חסכתם כ-[N] שעות

רוצה שנדבר 5 דקות על המשך?
```

### Day 14 — Conversion Call Script

1. **Open with data:**
   > "Here's what happened in your pilot: [X] posts, [Y] groups, [Z] CVs, [N] hours saved."

2. **Ask for reaction:**
   > "How does that compare to your previous process?"

3. **Present offer:**
   > "To continue, the Professional plan is 999 NIS/month — 3 recruiters, unlimited everything. As an early adopter, your first month is 499 NIS. And there's a money-back guarantee."

4. **Close:**
   > "Want to continue without a gap? I can activate your plan right now."

5. **If hesitation:**
   > "What would need to be different for you to continue?"
   > (Listen. Address. If not closeable, offer: "How about one more week at pilot terms while you decide?")

### If They Don't Convert

> "No problem at all. Your data stays in the system for 30 days if you change your mind. Can I ask — what was the main reason? And one more thing — know any other agencies that post to Facebook groups? I'd really appreciate an intro."
