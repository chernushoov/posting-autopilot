# First Wave Execution Plan

---

## Wave 1 Objective

**Start 2–3 pilots within 10 days.**

Everything in this plan is designed for one outcome: real agencies using the product within 2 weeks of starting outreach.

---

## Day-by-Day Plan

### Day 1 — Source (3 hours)

| Block | Task | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | Join 10 Facebook job groups. Scroll, identify agency posters. | 90 min | 12–15 leads |
| Midday | LinkedIn search for agencies. Cross-reference with FB findings. | 30 min | 5–8 leads |
| Afternoon | Google search + job boards for backfill. | 30 min | 3–5 leads |
| Late afternoon | Research contacts: find WhatsApp for top 10 leads. | 30 min | 8–10 contactable leads |
| End of day | Enter all leads into lead sheet. Score and tier them. | 15 min | Lead sheet populated |

**Day 1 exit: 20–25 leads in sheet. 10+ with WhatsApp. 8–10 Tier 1.**

---

### Day 2 — First Outreach (1.5 hours)

| Block | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | Send WhatsApp to Tier 1 leads (5 messages). Personalized — reference the FB group. | 20 min |
| 10:00 | Send WhatsApp to Tier 2 leads (5 messages). Qualifying approach. | 15 min |
| 11:00 | Send 3 cold emails to leads with email only. | 15 min |
| 14:00 | Check responses. Reply immediately to anyone who responded. | 15 min |
| 16:00 | Send WhatsApp to 3 more Tier 1 leads (if available). | 10 min |
| End of day | Update lead sheet statuses. Note next steps. | 10 min |

**Day 2 exit: 13–16 leads contacted. Expect 3–5 responses by end of Day 3.**

---

### Day 3 — Follow Up + Book Demos (1 hour)

| Block | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | Follow up on Day 2 WhatsApp — anyone who read but didn't reply. | 15 min |
| 10:00 | Respond to all replies. Qualify. Book demos for this week. | 20 min |
| 14:00 | Send 3–5 new outreach messages (remaining leads or Tier 2/3). | 15 min |
| 16:00 | Update lead sheet. Confirm demo times. | 10 min |

**Day 3 exit: 2–3 demos booked for Days 4–5. 5–8 conversations active.**

Follow-up message for non-responders:
```
היי [שם],
ראית את ההודעה שלי? 😊
אם מפרסמים בפייסבוק — שווה 5 דקות. אם לא — אין בעיה!
```

| Block | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|

| After each demo | Send follow-up WhatsApp with pilot offer. | 5 min each |
| Between demos | Continue outreach to remaining leads. | 20 min |
| End of Day 5 | Onboard any agency ready to start pilot (15 min each). | 15–30 min |

**Day 5 exit: 2–3 demos done. 1–2 pilots started or scheduled to start.**

| End of day | Update lead sheet. Plan next week. | 10 min |

## What Counts as Early Traction

### Green Signals (You're On Track)

| Signal | When | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 30%+ response rate on Tier 1 WhatsApp | Day 3 | Message resonates. ICP is right. |
| 2+ demos booked in first 5 days | Day 5 | Pipeline is moving. |
| First pilot started by Day 7 | Day 7 | Product is real enough to try. |
| Pilot client posts 3+ vacancies in first 3 days | Day 10 | Activation is working. |
| Pilot client says "this saves me time" | Day 10 | Value confirmed. |
| Someone asks "how much does this cost?" | Any day | Buying intent. |
| Someone refers you to another agency | Any day | Product-market fit signal. |

### Yellow Signals (Adjust)

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| <20% response rate | Message needs work or wrong targets | Try different WhatsApp script. Check if targets actually post to FB. |
| Demos booked but no-show | Low commitment | Send reminder 1 hour before. Offer shorter demo (5 min). |
| Pilot started but no posts after Day 2 | Activation failed | Call them. Do the first post together right now. |
| "Interesting but not now" from 3+ prospects | Timing or urgency problem | Strengthen "why now" framing. Try ban-risk angle. |

### Red Signals (Rethink)

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| <10% response rate after 20 messages | Wrong ICP or wrong channel | Stop. Research: are these people actually posting to FB? |
| 0 demos after 25 contacts | Offer not compelling | Stop. Talk to 3 recruiters informally. Ask what they'd pay for. |
| Pilot client stops after Day 1 | Product not delivering value | Stop. Call the client. Understand what went wrong. Fix before next pilot. |
| "We don't post to Facebook" from 5+ prospects | Wrong target segment | Shift sourcing to ONLY Facebook group posters (source 1). |
