# Screening Pack

Goal: filter quickly, not run long interviews.

## Must-have questions
1. In which city do you live, and can you reliably reach `[WORK LOCATION]`?
2. Do you have relevant experience for `[ROLE TITLE]`? If yes, how long and in what type of work?
3. Do you have a valid legal right to work in Israel?
4. When can you start?
5. Are you available for `[SCHEDULE / SHIFT TYPE]`?
6. Which language can you comfortably communicate in: Russian, Hebrew, or English?

## Optional questions
7. Do you have any limitation that would prevent you from doing this job safely and consistently?
8. Do you have any document or certificate that is required for this role?

## Quick filtering note
Skip fast if:
- candidate cannot legally work in Israel and this is mandatory;
- candidate cannot reach the work location reliably;
- candidate is unavailable for the required schedule;
- candidate can only start much later than the client needs;
- candidate clearly lacks the minimum fit for the role.

## Usage note
- Use only the 6 must-have questions unless the role genuinely needs the optional one.
- Do not ask for long background stories.
- Do not ask documents in chat unless absolutely necessary for the role.
