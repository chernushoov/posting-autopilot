# Facebook Safe Workflow — Product Definition

## What It Is

**Recruit Autopilot — Facebook Posting Assistant** is a compliance-safe vacancy distribution tool for Israeli recruitment agencies. It uses AI to generate localized, high-converting Facebook posts, organizes target groups into a searchable directory, and gives recruiters a streamlined manual-posting workflow with full tracking — without violating Facebook's Terms of Service.

It is NOT an autoposter. It is a **smart posting cockpit** that makes manual Facebook group posting 5x faster and fully trackable.

## What Problem It Solves

### The Recruiter's Reality
- Israeli recruiters post vacancies to 20–80 Facebook groups daily
- Each post must be localized, formatted, and adapted to group rules
- Tracking which groups were posted to, and which posts got results, is done manually (or not at all)
- The process takes 2–4 hours per recruiter per day

### The Autoposter Trap
- Many agencies use grey-market autoposters (browser extensions, scraping bots)
- Facebook actively detects and bans these tools
- Consequences: account bans, page bans, group bans, lost audience, lost ad accounts
- One agency lost access to 3 Facebook pages and 12 groups in a single enforcement wave
- There is no appeal process that reliably works

### What We Solve
1. **Speed** — AI generates ready-to-post content in seconds, not minutes
2. **Organization** — Group directory eliminates "which groups did I post to?"
3. **Tracking** — Every post is logged: where, when, what, with what result
4. **Safety** — Zero policy violations because the human always clicks "Post"
5. **Quality** — AI-generated posts outperform copy-paste templates

## Product Principles

1. **The human always posts** — we generate, organize, and track. The recruiter clicks "Post."
2. **Speed over automation** — reduce friction, don't remove the human
3. **Tracking is the moat** — autoposters can't track results; we can
4. **Group intelligence** — knowing which groups work is more valuable than posting to all of them
5. **Hebrew-first** — all content generation, UI copy, and defaults are Hebrew-native

---

## Competitive Positioning

### vs. Autoposters (browser extensions, bots)
- We are the safe alternative. They get you banned. We don't.

### vs. Manual posting (copy-paste from Word)
- We are 5x faster with better content and actual tracking.

### vs. Facebook Ads
- We are free distribution (organic groups). Ads cost money. We complement ads, not replace them.

### vs. Job boards (AllJobs, Drushim, etc.)
- Facebook groups reach passive candidates who never visit job boards.
- We are an additional channel, not a replacement.
